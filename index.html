What We Build

What We Build —
And How We Build It

01

Custom Home Builds

This is where it all starts — with a conversation about exactly what you want, and an honest plan to build it. Before anything gets framed, we walk your site, review every detail, and give you a fixed price and a real timeline. No vague estimates. No surprise change orders halfway through. You tell us the vision. We build it.

02

Luxury Remodels

We've spent years fixing remodels that were done wrong the first time. We know every shortcut that gets taken — because we've had to undo all of them. When BHR remodels your kitchen, bathroom, or entire home, we plan every detail before we start and do it right the first time. When we leave, the space is finished. Not mostly finished. Done.

03

Home Additions

An addition should look like it was always there. Same materials, same lines, same quality — not a box bolted onto the back of your house. We design and build additions that match your home so well that guests won't know where the original ended and the new space began.

04

Commercial Renovations

A delayed commercial project costs you money every day. We know that — so we plan it right, staff it right, and finish it. Tenant improvements and commercial builds done on time, on budget, and built to last. The same quality we bring to every custom home, applied to your commercial space.
